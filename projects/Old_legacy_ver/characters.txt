## Character Profiles:

**1. Elara Nightshade:**

- Role: Protagonist
- Age/Species: 19 Human
- Physical Description: Fair skin, midnight hair, emerald eyes, lithe and agile figure.
- Personality: Rebellious, resourceful, and fiercely independent. Possesses a rebellious spirit and unwavering determination to fight for justice.
- Background: Raised in a remote village ostracized by the Obsidian Circle.
- Motivations: To uncover the secrets of the Sundering and dismantle the Obsidian Circle's control.
- Skills/Abilities: Telepathy, shadow manipulation, illusion casting, acrobatics.

**2. Silas Whisperwood:**

- Role: Antagonist
- Age/Species: 26 Sylvan
- Physical Description: Tall, imposing figure with mossy hair and eyes of the deepest forest. Dresses in tattered, decaying garments.
- Personality: Obsessed with uncovering the secrets of the Void, driven by an insatiable thirst for power. Harbors a twisted love for chaos and destruction.
- Background: Descendant of the Whisperer King's illegitimate son.
- Motivations: To plunge Aethelra into eternal darkness and reshape it in his own image.
- Skills/Abilities: Telepathy, illusion projection, manipulation of shadows, corruption of others.

**3. Lumenfire:**

- Role: Supporting Character
- Age/Species: 20 Half-Elf
- Physical Description: Graceful half-elf with raven hair and emerald eyes. Known for her enchanting beauty and deadly skills.
- Personality: Fiercely independent, sarcastic, and skilled strategist. Possesses a deep love for balance and justice. Bisexual.
- Background: Trained by the Lumenborn resistance.
- Motivations: To restore balance to Aethelra and defeat the Void. When they meet she is infatuated and wishes to enter a bisexual relationship with Elara.
- Skills/Abilities: Shadow manipulation, healing, enchantment, illusion casting.
