## Character Profiles

**1. Lumenrose Whisperwood:**

- Role: Protagonist
- Physical Description: Tall, lithe figure with raven hair and pale skin, piercing emerald eyes that glow in low light.


**2. Silas Nightshade:**

- Role: Antagonist
- Physical Description: Stocky, scarred figure with pale, sickly skin and blood-red eyes.


**3. Elder Whisperer Fenya:**

- Role: Mentor / Deuteragonist
- Physical Description: Wispy, ethereal figure draped in luminescent fungi, eyes like twinkling stars.


**4. Talia Nightshade:**

- Role: Antagonistic Foil
- Physical Description: Beautiful, seductive figure with raven hair and pale skin, piercing crimson eyes.


**5. Lysander Moonlight:**

- Role: Supporting Character
- Physical Description: Rugged, scarred warrior with sun-bleached hair and piercing blue eyes.
